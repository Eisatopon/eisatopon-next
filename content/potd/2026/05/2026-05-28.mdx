hint: "A number is divisible by 11 if the alternating sum of its digits is divisible by 11. For 100...001, the digits are 1, then k zeros, then 1. Use the divisibility rule for 11: 10 ≡ -1 (mod 11), so 10^(k+1) + 1 ≡ (-1)^(k+1) + 1 (mod 11). This is 0 when k+1 is odd, i.e., k is even."
