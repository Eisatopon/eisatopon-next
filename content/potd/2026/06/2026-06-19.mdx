topic: "Combinatorics"
hint: "To guarantee at least one of each color, think about the worst case: you could pull out many socks without getting one of each color. The worst case is pulling out all socks of three colors before getting any of the fourth. Which color has the fewest socks? You need to exhaust all socks of the three most common colors and then pick one more."
---

You are in a completely dark room with a drawer containing 10 red, 20 blue, 30 green, and 40 khaki socks. What is the smallest number of socks you must randomly pull out in order to be sure of having at least one of each color?
