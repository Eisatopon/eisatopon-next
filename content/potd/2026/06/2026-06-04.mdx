If the angle between diameters is 30°, the circle S sits in a 30° wedge. Use the formula for a circle inscribed in an angle: its center is at distance r/sin(θ/2) from the vertex, where θ is the angle."

Let C be a circle with two diameters intersecting at an angle of 30 degrees. A circle S is tangent to both diameters and to C, and has radius 1. Find the largest possible radius of C.
